## Guide, host and review portraits

No stock portraits are assigned to named guides. The six static guide records in `src/data/public-site.ts` omit portrait URLs; guide cards and the booking host avatar show initials. `guide.image` and `rev.avatar` remain optional runtime data fields in the guide card, experience card, experience detail, and booking flow. No runtime account/guide uploads were available to verify identity or permission during this audit, so any such uploaded portrait is **UNVERIFIED** until the guide's identity and permission are confirmed. The initials fallback remains the compliant display for records without an authorized portrait. Static review data also contains no reviewer avatar URLs.
